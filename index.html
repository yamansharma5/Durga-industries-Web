About the company

Practical mechanical expertise for manufacturing businesses

Durga Industries provides practical mechanical engineering support for factories and industrial customers.

The company works with B2B customers including automobile, auto-parts, engineering, and general manufacturing businesses. Its work is centered on repair, maintenance, fabrication, machine modification, and custom part manufacturing for industrial machinery and production equipment.

Each enquiry starts with the customer requirement, available drawings or samples, and inspection where needed. Scope, quantity, timeline, and delivery expectations are confirmed before quotation.
